GEFIER R1

Compact Heavy-Payload Research Platform

The GEFIER R1 is a rugged, compact autonomous ground vehicle designed for all-terrain mobility and rapid hardware experimentation. Engineered with high-torque electric drive, all-terrain tires, and an onboard compute unit, the R1 excels in unstructured outdoor and GPS-denied environments.

  • Integrated with AutoMind Dynamics’ navigation stack for mapping, control, and obstacle avoidance
  • Engineered for both research and real-world experimentation, powered by open-source ROS2 frameworks
  • Modular payload support, enabling easy integration of custom sensors and research hardware
